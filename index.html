Our Story

Curated Gifts
With Love

Born from a passion for thoughtful gifting, we craft hampers that tell a story. Every ribbon tied and product chosen is a testament to our belief that the best gifts are deeply personal.

Meet The Maker

Hello, I'm Kamini.

What started as a small endeavor to create meaningful gifts for close friends quickly blossomed into Hampers By Me. I realized that in a world of quick clicks and instant deliveries, the art of genuine, considered gifting was getting lost.

I wanted to bring back the feeling of unboxing something truly special—where the packaging itself feels like a warm embrace. Every hamper we curate is assembled by hand in our small studio, ensuring that the love and intention behind your gift are palpable the moment it arrives.

"A gift isn't just an object; it's a feeling, perfectly packaged."

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does shipping take? keyboard_arrow_down
Standard shipping typically takes 3-5 business days within India. We also offer express delivery options. During festive seasons, please allow an extra 1-2 days for processing.
Can I create a fully custom hamper? keyboard_arrow_down
Absolutely! We love curating bespoke gifts. Reach out via email or WhatsApp with your budget, theme, and preferences, and we'll put together a custom proposal for you. Please allow 48 hours for custom quotes.
What is your return policy? keyboard_arrow_down
Due to the perishable and personalized nature of our hampers, we generally do not accept returns. However, if your hamper arrives damaged, please contact us within 24 hours with photos and we will make it right.
Do you offer corporate gifting? keyboard_arrow_down
Yes, we provide corporate gifting services including brand personalization and bulk shipping. Please email us at kamin4ikumari002@gmail.com to request our corporate brochure.
